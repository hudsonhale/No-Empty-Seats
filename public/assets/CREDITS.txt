The model is used with attribution as the artist-made centerpiece of the No Empty Seats concept. Its geometry, hand-painted textures, and animated objects are by Glen Fox. It has been decoded from Draco compression, normalized in scale, repositioned, and lit within a larger original environment. Original alley extensions, underground scenes, characters, agent choreography and application code are separate additions; Glen Fox did not create or endorse this application.
